North Carolina court resources
Which communities do you serve?

Our New Bern office serves clients in Craven, Carteret, Pamlico, and Jones counties and surrounding Eastern North Carolina communities. Call to discuss your matter and where it is being handled.

How do I arrange a consultation?

Call 252-631-1270 during office hours, Monday through Friday from 8 AM to 5 PM. The team can discuss your needs and help arrange the next step. The form on this concept website is for demonstration only.

Do you handle military legal matters?

Yes. Guy Smith’s practice includes military justice, with representation in courts-martial, administrative separation proceedings, investigations, and military record matters.

Where can I find courthouse information?

The North Carolina Judicial Branch maintains official location and contact information. Visit the Craven County Courthouse page ↗ or contact our office with questions about your appointment.
